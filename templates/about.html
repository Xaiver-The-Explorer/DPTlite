About DPTlite

Introduction

DPTlite (DNA Protein Toolbox, light version) is a non-profit bioinformatics web application focused on scientific research and educational purposes.

Ambition

It is built to help college students and curious minds worldwide see

  • how a nucleic acid sequence becomes a protein
  • how a protein's own composition hints at its shape
through simple, transparent heuristics rather than a black box.
